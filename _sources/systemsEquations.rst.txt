
System of Equations in Two Variables
===========================================

Consider a system of equations in two variables with a uniques solution

.. math::

   & 7x-2y=45

   & 5x+y=37

The solution for :math:`x` can be written down directly as

.. math::

   x=\frac{(45)(1)-(37)(-2)}{(7)(1)-(5)(-2)}=\frac{119}{17}=7

We are using cross-multiplication and subtraction.


:math:`y` can then be solved by substituting :math:`x` in any of the two equations.

In general, if a unique solution exists, then

.. math::

   & a_1 x+b_1 y = c_1

   & a_2 x+b_2 y = c_2

   & \implies

   & x = \frac{c_1 b_2 - c_2 b_1}{a_1 b_2 - a_2 b_1}

   & y = \frac{c_1 a_2 - c_2 a_1}{b_1 a_2 - b_2 a_1}~\text{(Interchanging $a$ and $b$ in the result for $x$)}
